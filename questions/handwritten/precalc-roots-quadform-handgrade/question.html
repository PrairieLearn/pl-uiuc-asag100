Correct solution for reference

  1. Set $f(x)=0$ and factor out $x$.

    \[ {{params.f}}=0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad {{params.factored_equation}}. \]

    By the zero-product property, \(x=0\) or \({{params.quadratic}}=0\).

  2. For the quadratic, use $a=5$, $b=-1$, and $c={{params.c}}$. The discriminant is

    \[ \Delta=b^2-4ac=(-1)^2-4(5)({{params.c}})={{params.D}}. \]

    Substitute into the quadratic formula.

    \[ x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a} \] \[ {{params.quadratic_setup}} =\frac{1\pm\sqrt{ {{params.D}} }}{10}. \]

  3. Include both quadratic roots and the root from the factor $x$.

    \[ x=0,\quad x={{params.root_plus}},\quad x={{params.root_minus}}. \]
