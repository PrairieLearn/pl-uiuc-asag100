Correct solution for reference

  1. Use the formula for the projection of $\vec{a}$ onto $\vec{b}$.

    \[ \vec{c}=\frac{\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}}{\vec{b}\cdot\vec{b}}\,\vec{b}. \]

  2. Compute the dot products from the vector components.

    \[ \vec{a}\cdot\vec{b} =\big[{{params.a_dot_b_work}}\big]\,\mathrm{m}^2 ={{correct_answers.a_dot_b}}\,\mathrm{m}^2. \] \[ \vec{b}\cdot\vec{b} =\big[{{params.b_dot_b_work}}\big]\,\mathrm{m}^2 ={{correct_answers.b_dot_b}}\,\mathrm{m}^2. \]

  3. Multiply $\vec{b}$ by the ratio of the dot products.

    \[ \begin{aligned} \vec{c} &= \frac{ {{correct_answers.a_dot_b}} }{ {{correct_answers.b_dot_b}} } \left({{params.b_vec}}\right)\,\mathrm{m}\\ &= {{correct_answers.c_vec}}\,\mathrm{m}. \end{aligned} \]

The projection $\vec{c} = \operatorname{Proj}(\vec{a}, \vec{b})$ is given by the formula: $$\vec{c} = \frac{\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}}{|\vec{b}|^2} \vec{b}$$

First, we need to calculate the dot product $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$: $$\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = {{correct_answers.a_dot_b}}$$

Next, we calculate the magnitude squared of $\vec{b}$: $$|\vec{b}|^2 = {{correct_answers.b_dot_b}}$$

Now we can find the projection $\vec{c}$: $$\begin{aligned} \vec{c} &= \frac{\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}}{|\vec{b}|^2} \vec{b} \\ &= \frac{ {{correct_answers.a_dot_b}} }{ {{correct_answers.b_dot_b}} } ({{params.b_vec}}) \\ &= {{correct_answers.c_vec}}{\rm\ m} \end{aligned}$$
